## Key Observations

- **SA** produced the highest measured yield strength: 812 MPa.
- **CA1B** produced the highest measured UTS: 2101 MPa.
- **CA2B** produced the highest hardness: 48.66 HRC / 506 HV.
- **DA2B** produced the highest elongation: 19.80%.
- **DA2B** produced the highest impact energy: 60.97 J.
- **AR** exhibited the lowest measured corrosion rate: 0.145 mm/year.

The results demonstrate that the optimum heat-treatment condition depends on the target material property rather than a single universally optimal treatment.
